# Brag Plan: AMAZONIA

## What is this app?
An interactive nature documentary where scrolling controls an 80-second continuous cinematic flight through the Amazon rainforest across 8 distinct primeval biome sectors.

## The angle
National Geographic meets Active Theory: an uncompromising, museum-grade digital flight experience where every pixel communicates the wild beauty of the forest through restraint, real-time telemetry, and zero-latency scroll scrubbing.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Pure cinematic sunrise over the endless canopy with drifting mist. The Macaw feather line drawing draws on screen and transforms into the flight recorder HUD ribbon as the headline "AMAZONIA" emerges in organic Fraunces serif.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The River Glide:** Sweeping low over the mighty Amazon river with pink dolphins breaching, driven directly by fluid scroll scrubbing.
- **Real-Time Telemetry & Gyro Navigation:** Live HUD displaying coordinates, 44 KT airspeed, active heading, and the River Current progress rail tracing the flight.
- **The Night Transformation:** Daylight dissolves into the bioluminescent night with drifting fireflies and the authentic embossed brass & emerald Expedition Seal medallion.
- **Expedition Dossier:** Interactive scientific dossier revealing biome specs, acoustic canopy layers, and telemetry.

## Outro / punchline
The drone pulls back into the star-filled river basin. The National Geographic gold frame locks with the Expedition Seal: "THE PRIMEVAL FOREST AWAITS. BEGIN YOUR EXPEDITION."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Calibrate:** Preloader draws feather motif and connects telemetry.
2. **Scrub:** User scrolls seamlessly through 8 biomes with zero-latency All-Intra video scrubbing.
3. **Explore:** User jumps through waypoints and accesses the scientific Expedition Dossier.

## Audio direction
- Role: Cinematic support bed with lush low swell, organic rainforest atmosphere, and restrained brass/chime audio hits.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (cinematic groove with crisp percussion).
- Music treatment: Low atmospheric opening, rhythmic swell during the river flight, glittering sparkle during the night transformation, and resonant closing resolve.
- Music cue guidance: Strong cue locks at scene transitions (3.5s, 8.5s, 14.5s).
- Audio-reactive treatment: Subtle breathing glow on the HUD telemetry and expedition seal border.
- SFX posture: Sparse and tasteful — delicate camera optic calibration click, soft water ripple, glass ping on waypoint jump.
- Restraint rule: Never overpower the narration or footage with harsh synthetic noises.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Canopy Awakening — 3.5s
- **Visuals:** Sunrise mist over emergent kapok trees. Drone optics calibration HUD animates on. "AMAZONIA" typography emerges with elegant letter-spacing expansion.
- **Text:** "AMAZONIA" / "AN 80-SECOND CINEMATIC FLIGHT" / "AERIAL SURVEY · 03°08'42.1\"S 60°01'38.4\"W"
- **Sequential:** Calibration status ticks `0% → 100%`, HUD ribbon snaps to green.
- **Audio intent:** Mysterious, awe-inspiring opening swell.
- **Transition mood:** Dramatic forward push → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Fluid Scroll Scrubbing — 5.0s
- **Visuals:** Low flight skimming the Amazon river with pink river dolphins. Interactive mouse wheel / scroll gesture sweeps forward. Live telemetry ribbon clocks airspeed (44 KT) and heading (284° WNW).
- **Text:** "CONTROLLED ENTIRELY BY SCROLL" / "ZERO KEYFRAME LATENCY · ALL-INTRA 1080P"
- **Sequential:** River Current SVG path illuminates downward with progress indicator.
- **Audio intent:** Rhythmic drive and fluid motion.
- **Transition mood:** Dynamic swipe / crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — 8 Biomes & The Expedition Seal — 6.0s
- **Visuals:** Scene shifts into sunset and the bioluminescent night. Glowing teal fireflies drift across the screen. The authentic brass and emerald Expedition Medallion rotates into place in the corner. Expedition Dossier modal opens with interactive telemetry.
- **Text:** "8 PRIMEVAL BIOMES" / "EXPEDITION DOSSIER & SCIENTIFIC TELEMETRY"
- **Sequential:** Waypoint buttons `01` through `08` illuminate sequentially.
- **Audio intent:** Magical, sparkling nocturnal atmosphere.
- **Transition mood:** Soft cinematic fade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Primeval Call (Outro) — 5.5s
- **Visuals:** Wide drone pullback over the winding Amazon river under starlight. Glowing gold National Geographic border with the official Amazon Rainforest Aerial Survey Seal.
- **Text:** "THE PRIMEVAL FOREST AWAITS" / "BEGIN YOUR EXPEDITION" / "LIVE AT AMAZONIA"
- **Sequential:** CTA button glows with bioluminescent aura.
- **Audio intent:** Grand harmonic resolution and lingering warm resonance.
